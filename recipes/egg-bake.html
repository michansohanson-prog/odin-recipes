Bacon Spinach Egg-Bake

The best Egg-Bake!

This is an often-requested favorite dish for Easter. It's certainly not healthy, but it's delicious and very easy to make. A crustless egg-bake with simple, fresh ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 10 eggs
  • 1 stick of butter
  • 1 package of bacon
  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 big handful of spinach
  • 6 cups of shredded cheese (3 bags)
  • half cup of large curd cottage cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the bacon - a little crispy is perfect.
  2. Crack the 10 eggs in a large bowl.
  3. Melt the butter and add to the bowl.
  4. Add the flour and baking powder.
  5. Mix it all up!
  6. Chop up the bacon and spinach and add to the bowl.
  7. Mix it up again!
  8. Add all of the shredded cheese and cottage cheese.
  9. Mix it up again!
  10. Lightly grease a recangular baking dish
  11. Bake at 400o f for 15 minutes, then turn the temp to 350. Don't open the door.
  12. Remove from the oven when it's golden brown on top and the middle is just barely set.
  13. Let it rest for at least 10 minutes.
  14. You're welcome.
