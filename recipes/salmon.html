Easy Salmon Filet

My Wife's favorite salmon recipe

This recipe goes pan-to-oven, so you need a good pan that you can use for both. I prefer cast-iron, but quality stainless steel is great, too. We make a delicious crust on the flesh first, then bake it the rest of the way for a juicy finish.

Ingredients

  • Salmon Filet - skin on
  • avocado oil
  • salt
  • pepper
  • garlic powder
  • onion powder
  • butter

Steps

  1. Dry the salmon really well with paper towels.
  2. season with salt, pepper, onion powder, and garlio powder.
  3. Heat the pan up good and hot. Add the avocado oil, then turn the head down a touch.
  4. Add the salmon: flesh side down. Let it sit and develop a rich brown color.
  5. Gently flip, toss in a bunch of butter, and chuck it in the oven.
  6. Bake at 400o f for about 7 or 8 minutes.
  7. Tasty fish!
